Edition Theme // Transcending Timelines

Chasing Euphoria.

🎬 OFFICIAL TEASER TRAILER
"THE NIGHT WE NEVER LEFT"

Where Freedom and Thrill Collide Under the Blood Moon

Chasing Euphoria is about chasing those moments that make you feel truly alive—the rush when music is deafening, stage lights blur into mist, and nothing else matters. It's bold, emotional, and unapologetically expressive, blending nocturnal gothic aesthetics with high-octane celebration.

From unforgettable performances by Diljit Dosanjh, Vishal-Shekhar, Mohit Chauhan, Zakir Khan, and Samay Raina to the fiercest collegiate rivalries in North India, Zeitgeist creates a world where euphoria isn't a place you reach, but a feeling you experience together.
